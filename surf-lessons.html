Why Wave & Zen

The Benefits of Private Surf Coaching

Traditional surf schools group up to 8 students per instructor. We focus entirely on you, delivering a safe, accelerated, and personalized experience.

Certified ISA Coaching

Learn from International Surfing Association (ISA) Level 2 coaches who specialize in video analysis and personalized bio-mechanic feedback.

Video Technique Review

We capture HD footage of your rides, breaking down your paddle timing, popup technique, and stance mechanics in detailed dry-land debriefs.

Premium Custom Equipment

No one-size-fits-all foamies. We match you with premium epoxy or high-density softboards tailored specifically to your weight, height, and style.

Safety & Ocean Reading

We teach you how to read swell charts, spot rip currents, negotiate beach breaks, and navigate lineups confidently and safely on your own.

Daily Rhythm

Hourly Surf Coaching Windows

Waves shift with the tide. To guarantee optimal surfing conditions, we schedule our private coaching during three peak daily windows.

06:00 – 08:30
Dawn Patrol Session
Light offshore winds and clean glass lines. Ideal for intermediate pop-up training and advanced trim drills.
Clean Swell
09:00 – 11:30
Mid-Morning Session
Rising high tide, soft beach breaks. Perfect for beginners learning positioning, board control, and riding first waves.
Beginner Friendly
15:30 – 18:00
Sunset Glass Session
Fading winds, long ocean rollers. Beautiful lighting and comfortable temperatures. Great for relaxed styling and cruise surfing.
Scenic Cruise
