Stay Safe Out There

Ocean Safety Zone Guide

Understanding your conditions helps you make better decisions in the water. Always check your zone before entering.

Green Zone — All Clear

Swell under 1.5m, light offshore winds, water temp 20–26°C. Ideal for beginners and first-time surfers. All levels welcome.

Blue Zone — Intermediate

Swell 1.5–2.5m with moderate winds. Best for intermediate surfers; beginners should remain near shore with an instructor.

Yellow Zone — Advanced Only

Swell 2.5–3.5m with stronger currents. Experienced surfers only; always surf with a partner and notify a lifeguard.

Red Zone — Do Not Enter

Swell above 3.5m, strong rip currents, dangerous reef conditions. No water entry permitted. Sessions will be rescheduled.
