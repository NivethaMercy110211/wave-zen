The Benefits of Beach Yoga

Flowing on the sand next to the ocean provides sensory grounding, fresh sea air to oxygenate the body, and a natural rhythm to sync your breathing.

Sunrise Beach Setting

Start your day directly on the sand, breathing in clean ocean breezes and absorbing natural Vitamin D as the sun rises over the horizon.

Certified Yoga RYT
