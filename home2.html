Your Daily Retreat Flow

Experience the perfect balance of action, mindfulness, and coastal relaxation from dawn to dusk.

06:30 AM
Sunrise Beach Yoga Flow

Awaken your senses with a gentle, invigorating Vinyasa flow directly on the sand. Breathe in the cool sea breeze as the golden sun rises over the horizon.

08:30 AM
Organic Shoreline Breakfast

Re-energize with fresh tropical fruits, organic acai bowls, locally sourced juices, and freshly brewed coffee overlooking the waves.

10:00 AM
1-on-1 Personalized Surf Coaching

Head into the water with your dedicated instructor. Master wave selection, perfect your pop-up, and experience the thrill of riding pristine swells.

01:00 PM
Catered Beachside Lunch & Siesta

Enjoy a delicious, chef-prepared lunch focusing on healthy, seasonal seafood and plant-based dishes, followed by free time to lounge or read in a hammock.

03:30 PM
Guided Free Surf or Coastline Hike

Practice your surf skills with coaches monitoring from the shore, or explore secret tide pools and sandy cliffs on a guided coastal nature walk.

06:00 PM
Sunset Meditation & Bonfire Gathering

Wind down with yin yoga and mindfulness meditation as the stars appear, followed by fresh organic cocktails and acoustic music around a beach bonfire.
