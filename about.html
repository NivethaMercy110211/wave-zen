Our Mission

We Teach the Ocean,
the Ocean Teaches You.

Wave & Zen was born from a simple belief: the best classroom has no walls. Founded in 2014, we've spent a decade helping people find joy, courage, and peace in the rhythm of the sea.

Book a Session Meet Our Team
The Beginning
"The ocean doesn't care about your resume — it teaches you to be fully, completely present."

It started with Marcus Chen standing knee-deep in the Pacific, watching a first-time student pop up on their board and scream with absolute joy. He knew then that surf coaching wasn't just about sport — it was about transformation.

Elena Vasquez joined shortly after, bringing her decade of coastal yoga practice to complement the surfing side. Together, they built Wave & Zen as a sanctuary where physical challenge and mindful movement intersect.

Today our team of 12 certified coaches operates across 6 global beach locations, having guided over 2,400 students to their breakthrough moment on the water.

What We Stand For

Our Four Core Values

Everything we teach, plan, and deliver flows from these foundational beliefs.

Authenticity

We teach real ocean skills through honest, supportive coaching — no shortcuts or gimmicks. Just genuine progress in a natural coastal setting.

Safety First

Each session starts with a safety check. We monitor conditions and ocean safety. Every student stays protected during the full experience.

Love for Nature

We minimize beach impact, use reef-safe products, and teach our community practical ways to protect and conserve the ocean.

Holistic Wellness

Body, mind, breath — we integrate physical ocean activity with mindfulness practices to deliver truly transformative experiences.

How We Teach

Our Proven Teaching Methodology

A structured approach that respects each student's pace while maximizing learning and confidence.

Step 01

Read the Ocean First

Before you ever touch the water, we teach you to observe. Understanding currents, wave sets, and tide patterns makes you a safer, smarter surfer from day one.

Our onshore briefings use visual aids, real-time conditions, and years of local knowledge to prepare you for what's ahead.

See Live Conditions →
Step 02

Progress at Your Own Pace

Our skill-level assessment means no student is ever rushed into conditions beyond their ability. We use a personalized progression map for each student.

From pop-up technique and paddling to duck dives and riding green waves — every milestone is celebrated and documented.
