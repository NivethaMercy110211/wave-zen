ISA Level 2 Surf Coach · Co-Founder

Marcus has been surfing since age 8 and coaching professionally for over 14 years. With stints in Hawaii, Costa Rica, and Portugal, he brings a truly global ocean perspective to every lesson.

His coaching philosophy is patient, method-driven, and always encouraging. He believes every person — regardless of age, fitness, or background — can experience the pure joy of riding a wave.
